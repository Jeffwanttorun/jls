## Verification standard

This record distinguishes between:

- evidence that directly supports a claim in a defined Wuyishan study or protected-area context;
- evidence that supports only a narrower version of the claim; and
- evidence that provides useful context but does not prove the full wording.

Official descriptions are used for the World Heritage property or Wuyishan National Park only within the boundaries they describe. A study of one reserve, elevation range, taxonomic group, or set of sample plots is not treated as evidence for every habitat or species in the wider Wuyishan area.

## Verified source records

### NH-S1 — Mount Wuyi

- **Title:** Mount Wuyi
- **Publisher / institution:** UNESCO World Heritage Centre
- **URL:** https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/911/
- **Publication date:** Not stated on the page. The property was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 1999.
- **Access date:** 2026-07-15
- **Supports:** NH-04, NH-06, NH-10; contextual support for NH-08
- **Exact supported scope:** The World Heritage Centre describes the property as containing a major, largely intact example of Chinese subtropical forest and South Chinese rainforest, notes its high plant and animal diversity, and records ongoing monitoring and research on the subtropical forest ecosystem and biodiversity protection.
- **Limitations:** This is a World Heritage property overview, not a habitat-by-habitat scientific study. It does not directly establish the article's full NH-03 statement about forests, streams, and elevation, or the broad NH-05 phrase “different forms of life.” Its scope should not automatically be extended to every administrative or geographic use of “Wuyishan.”

### NH-S2 — 公园简介 (Park Introduction)

- **Title:** 公园简介
- **Publisher / institution:** 武夷山国家公园福建管理局 (Fujian Administration of Wuyishan National Park)
- **URL:** https://wysgjgy.fj.gov.cn/gygk/gyjj/
- **Publication date:** Not stated on the page.
- **Access date:** 2026-07-15
- **Supports:** NH-03, NH-04, NH-05, NH-06, NH-10
- **Exact supported scope:** The official overview identifies the park as part of the mid-subtropical evergreen broadleaf forest region. It describes vertical climatic variation and multiple vegetation belts associated with elevation, temperature, and precipitation. It also records decades of field survey, specimen collection, identification work, and biological inventory activity.
- **Limitations:** This is an official overview rather than a peer-reviewed methods paper. Its claims apply to the national park scope described by the agency. Inventory figures may change as surveys continue. Promotional or superlative language on the page should not be carried into the article. The page supports variation in vegetation and ecological settings more directly than it supports a general statement about all “forms of life.”

### NH-S4 — Variation in Niche and Interspecific Associations across Elevations in Subtropical Forest Communities of the Wuyi Mountains, Southeastern China

- **Title:** Variation in Niche and Interspecific Associations across Elevations in Subtropical Forest Communities of the Wuyi Mountains, Southeastern China
- **Authors:** Jintao Hu, Zhaoliang Zheng, Xinyi Wen, Xisheng Hu, Yongming Lin, Jian Li, Jian Ni, and Chengzhen Wu
- **Publisher / institution:** *Forests* (MDPI), Volume 15, Issue 7, Article 1256
- **URL:** https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4907/15/7/1256
- **Publication date:** 2024-07-19
- **Access date:** 2026-07-15
- **Supports:** NH-03, NH-04, NH-05, NH-06, NH-08; partial support for NH-10
- **Exact supported scope:** The study examines woody plant communities in 34 plots across an elevation range in the Wuyi Mountain National Nature Reserve. It documents changes in forest type, dominant species, niche characteristics, and interspecific associations across elevation, and provides direct evidence of ecological research in the Wuyi Mountains.
- **Limitations:** This is one study of woody plants in a defined reserve, elevation range, and plot system. It does not represent every habitat, organism, stream, or part of the wider Wuyishan region. Background statements in the introduction should not be treated as the study's own findings unless their cited sources are checked separately.

## Claim-to-source mapping

### NH-03 — Different habitats exist within mountain environments

- **Mapped evidence:** NH-S2 documents multiple elevation-related vegetation belts and varied ecological settings. NH-S4 documents distinct forest types and woody plant communities across an elevation gradient.
- **What is verified:** Mountain environments within the official park or studied reserve contain differentiated vegetation or forest-community zones.
- **What is not verified by these sources:** The article's combined causal statement that forests, streams, and elevation together create different habitats throughout Wuyishan.
- **Decision:** **Needs wording reduction.** Restrict the future wording to differences in vegetation or forest communities across elevation and environmental gradients unless a separate source verifies stream-related habitat formation.

### NH-04 — Wuyishan includes subtropical forest landscapes

- **Mapped evidence:** NH-S1 identifies important subtropical forest within the World Heritage property. NH-S2 places the national park within the mid-subtropical evergreen broadleaf forest region. NH-S4 studies subtropical forest communities in the Wuyi Mountain National Nature Reserve.
- **What is verified:** Subtropical forest is a documented characteristic of the World Heritage property, national park, and studied reserve.
- **Decision:** **Supported.** Preserve a clear geographic scope in future wording.

### NH-05 — Different environmental conditions support different forms of life

- **Mapped evidence:** NH-S2 links vertical changes in elevation, temperature, and precipitation with multiple vegetation belts. NH-S4 documents forest-type and woody plant-community differences across elevation.
- **What is verified:** Environmental and elevational gradients correspond with documented differences in vegetation or woody plant communities.
- **What is not verified by these sources:** A general claim covering all forms of life or every environmental condition in Wuyishan.
- **Decision:** **Needs wording reduction.** Use the narrower, directly supported plant-community or vegetation formulation.

### NH-06 — Wuyishan has scientific research value

- **Mapped evidence:** NH-S1 records continuing ecological monitoring and research. NH-S2 documents long-term survey and specimen work. NH-S3 records research collaborations, projects, and a biodiversity baseline investigation. NH-S4 is a peer-reviewed example of current ecological research.
- **What is verified:** The protected areas associated with Wuyishan are active settings for biodiversity surveys, ecological monitoring, and scientific study.
- **What is not established by this verification:** A complete history of Wuyishan research, its earliest research activity, or a comparative claim that it is more scientifically important than other regions.
- **Decision:** **Supported within the documented scope.**

### NH-08 — Natural landscapes can support biodiversity understanding and ecological research

- **Mapped evidence:** NH-S3 documents a biodiversity baseline investigation and related research. NH-S4 uses field plots and environmental measurements to examine species diversity and ecological relationships across elevation.
- **What is verified:** Wuyishan forest landscapes have been used for biodiversity inventory and ecological research.
- **What is not verified by these sources:** A universal proposition about every natural landscape or every research topic listed in the earlier mapping.
- **Decision:** **Supported in a narrower form.** Use Wuyishan-specific research examples rather than a universal statement.

### NH-10 — Wuyishan's natural value includes biodiversity and scientific interest

- **Mapped evidence:** NH-S1 supports the biodiversity importance of the World Heritage property. NH-S1, NH-S2, NH-S3, and NH-S4 separately document monitoring, inventory work, and scientific research.
- **What is verified:** Biodiversity significance and continuing scientific activity are both documented within defined Wuyishan protected-area contexts.
- **What is not established by this verification:** An unrestricted claim about every part of Wuyishan, or a ranking against other natural sites.
- **Decision:** **Supported**, provided the biodiversity and research aspects retain their separate evidence and geographic scope.
