# Project Architecture

## Rendering model

Astro generates static HTML. The only visitor-facing JavaScript is a small inline mobile-navigation enhancement for Escape-to-close and accurate `aria-expanded` state. Content pages require no hydration.

## Content systems

- `guides`: established top-level guides and category pages.
- `locations`: typed location records plus separate Markdown narratives.
- `people`: future local-resident and interview profiles; the collection remains empty until real source material is supplied.
- `routes`: future route articles.
- `teaArticles`: future tea culture articles.
- `familyGuides`: future family-focused guides.
- `localStories`: future first-person or reported local stories.
- `knowledgeArticles`: one unified collection for Natural, Cultural, Tea, and Living Heritage.
- `src/data/videos.ts`: future YouTube and Instagram assets, transcript metadata, publishing state, and typed relationships; the registry is empty and has no public route.
- `src/data/creator-profiles.ts`: confirmed creator-channel/profile links for future About or homepage use; the registry is empty and creates no public links.
- `src/data/place-sources/`: automatically discovered JSON facts for future places; existing typed location files remain supported.
- `src/data/video-sources/`: automatically discovered JSON assets for future videos.
- `src/data/image-sources/`: automatically discovered metadata for future managed images; originals and generated variants remain separate.

The future editorial collections share metadata, image, trust, translation, and publishing fields from `src/content/schema.ts`. Their pages are generated by one route and one editorial layout.

`knowledgeArticles` is the primary editorial pillar. It supports a cross-theme overview plus four structural heritage categories in the visitor-facing order Natural, Tea, Cultural, and Living Heritage. Explore collections are practical; People and Stories are supporting context. This hierarchy affects editorial organization, not the shared rendering system.

People, routes, tea articles, family guides, and local stories use the same shared editorial fields. These include optional names, introductions, personal-experience notes, practical information, photos, related videos, related places, related stories, source status, field-check date, and author information.

## Strategy-to-system mapping

- Why Wuyishan uses the unified `knowledgeArticles` collection, the `overview` category and four heritage categories, historical context, sources, controlled topic IDs, optional visitor-focused `whyItMatters` copy, and a small controlled `keyIdeaIds` learning overview.
- Explore Wuyishan uses the existing guide, location, route, and family systems without changing their current URLs. `experience-categories.ts` provides controlled visitor perspectives and maps them to existing pages or Explore sections without creating empty category pages.
- Stories of Wuyishan uses People and Local Stories as supporting material, with `relatedThemes` connecting an interview to a larger subject.
- About Jeff remains the existing `/about` page and Person structured data.

Pillar definitions live in `src/data/pillars.ts`. `PillarLayout` provides the shared page shell, metadata, heading structure, optional introduction slot, and published-content cards. `PillarSection` provides sections that can connect existing content or describe a reviewed future direction without creating an empty child page.

Location records support an English name, verified Chinese name, description, significance, practical information, Markdown personal notes, related topics, and related stories. People records support interview metadata, story body, video, related places, and related themes.

## Current technical boundaries

- The primary navigation reflects the long-term hierarchy: Home, Why Wuyishan, Explore Wuyishan, Stories of Wuyishan, and About Jeff. Contact remains a footer utility.
- Relationships are entered once with stable IDs; the unified registry generates reverse lookup without automatically rendering backlinks.
- Sources are listed at article level rather than attached to individual sentences.
- The file-based system has no CMS workflow, search index, or automated source verification.
- Existing URLs remain unchanged; future hierarchy should use hub pages and redirects rather than breaking links.
- Public pillar routes organize existing content without moving or removing established URLs. They do not generate empty People, Stories, Routes, Videos, or topic pages.

## Relationship validation

- Heritage topics come from `src/data/topics.ts`; collection schemas reject unknown topic IDs.
- Related place, person, and story references must use internal paths. Generated-site validation rejects broken links on published pages.
- Video records validate platforms, URLs, publication requirements, image metadata, transcript sources, duplicate slugs, and duplicate relationships. Reverse and relationship-specific lookups are available for future related-media views.
- Reverse topic lookup is available without generating public topic pages.
- Cross-collection reverse links are prepared but not automatically rendered.

`buildSiteContentRegistry` is the authoritative cross-content index. It combines knowledge articles, places, routes, people, stories, topics, and videos; merges locale-specific labels; validates targets; and provides reverse lookup. New content uses stable ID arrays while legacy label-and-URL objects remain readable during the compatibility period.

Managed images also enter this registry as non-page nodes. Their place, topic, article, and video IDs are validated without creating image pages or sitemap entries.

## Empty collection behavior

Future collections do not contain `_template.md` or schema-placeholder records. `markdownLoader` returns an empty content loader until a real Markdown file exists, and the dynamic route skips empty directories before calling Astro's collection API. Editable examples remain outside the content tree in `content-templates/`.

## Publishing states

- `draft`: validated during development but omitted from public routes.
- `published`: rendered as a public static page and included in the sitemap.

Location records use the same concept through `recordStatus` in the typed registry.

Development-only routes under `/draft-preview/` render drafts with a visible status banner and `noindex`. Their `getStaticPaths` functions return an empty list outside `import.meta.env.DEV`. Public route and directory queries also require `recordStatus: published`, providing a separate production safeguard. Preview routes support guides, editorial collections, places, and videos without adding public pages.

## Reusable presentation

- `BaseLayout`: document metadata, canonical URL, social metadata, global structured data, header, and footer.
- `PillarLayout` and `PillarSection`: shared presentation for the public Why Wuyishan, Explore Wuyishan, and Stories of Wuyishan pillar pages.
- `GuideLayout`: established guide articles.
- `EditorialLayout`: people and interviews, routes, tea culture, family guides, and local stories.
- `LocationPage`: all location pages.
- `LocationDetails`, `TrustLabel`, `PhotoGallery`, `MapSection`, `VideoCard`, `VideoEmbed`, `VideoMetadata`, `RelatedVideos`, and `CreatorProfiles`: place, media, and optional creator-profile modules.
- `ManagedImage`: responsive AVIF, WebP, and JPEG output from a validated image asset record.
- `MapExplorer`: future Leaflet map with localized datasets, place and topic filters, verified route geometry, and a list fallback; it is not used by a public page yet.
- `EditorialExtras`, `PracticalInformation`, `LinkedVideos`, `RelatedContent`, and `AuthorInfo`: optional shared modules that render only when structured content is present.
- `TopicList`: resolves canonical topic IDs without linking to nonexistent topic pages.

Reusable structured-data helpers cover Article, Person, Place, VideoObject, and BreadcrumbList. `videoRecordSchema` prepares reviewed video assets for VideoObject output, while `getVideoSocialMetadata` prepares their future Open Graph inputs. Person data accepts confirmed creator profiles through `sameAs`, but none are emitted while the creator-profile registry is empty. Location pages use the shared Place helper, People entries add a Person entity alongside their Article data, and About Jeff uses the shared Person entity builder.

## Validation and failure behavior

`npm run build` runs Astro’s type and content checks before static generation. The build fails when:

- a content record violates its schema;
- a slug has an invalid format;
- location slugs are duplicated;
- a published location has no matching narrative;
- a configured image lacks alt text or dimensions.
- a generated page lacks a title, description, canonical URL, or exactly one H1;
- generated titles, descriptions, or canonical URLs are duplicated;
- social images or social-image alt text are missing;
- JSON-LD is invalid, or robots.txt does not point to the production sitemap;
- a generated image lacks dimensions or alt text;
- a generated internal link points to a missing static page.
- a managed image has missing metadata, invalid dimensions, an oversized file, a duplicate filename, a missing derivative, unconfirmed published rights, or a broken relationship.

Editorial collections support an optional `lastPersonallyChecked` field. It remains absent unless Jeff confirms an in-person check. The build rejects `checked-on-location` status when that date is missing.

Unknown travel facts are not validation errors. They remain absent and are displayed as requiring Jeff’s confirmation.

## SEO behavior

Public pages receive canonical URLs from `SITE_URL`, Open Graph and X metadata, semantic headings, and JSON-LD where appropriate. Article and location routes add breadcrumb data. `@astrojs/sitemap` generates `sitemap-index.xml`; `robots.txt` points to it.

## Bilingual growth

English remains the default and uses unprefixed URLs. A reviewed Chinese counterpart uses a separate Markdown file with `lang: zh`, the same `translationKey`, and a `/zh/` URL. The Editorial route now generates language alternates and rejects duplicate language records for one translation key. Do not publish empty or machine-only translations.
