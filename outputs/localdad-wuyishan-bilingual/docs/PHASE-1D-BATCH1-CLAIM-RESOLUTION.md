**Prepared:** 2026-07-15

## Resolution classes

- **A — Keep and verify with source:** The claim is important to the article and should remain only after suitable evidence is mapped to it.
- **B — Rewrite as softer interpretation:** The idea is useful, but it should be framed as interpretation, a question, or general visitor guidance rather than a strong factual claim.
- **C — Remove or postpone:** The claim is not necessary for the article's core purpose, or it requires evidence that is not currently available. Keep it out of a publication candidate until the missing evidence exists.
- **D — Convert to Jeff firsthand observation:** Use only after Jeff explicitly confirms the observation, its scope, and any relevant date or place.

No claim is assigned to D in this plan. The repository contains no explicit Jeff confirmation for these claims. A future confirmation may justify a separate, approved reclassification; it must not be inferred from Jeff living in Wuyishan.

## Priority definitions

- **High:** Central to the article's purpose or a publication blocker if unsupported.
- **Medium:** Important supporting context, but the article can be narrowed if evidence remains unavailable.
- **Low:** Optional interpretation or guidance that can be postponed without weakening the core article.
