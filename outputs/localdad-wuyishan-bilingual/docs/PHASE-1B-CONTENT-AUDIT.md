## Existing public content outside the one-to-one inventory

`/wuyi-rock-tea` remains an established visitor guide. It is not treated as a substitute for either the craftsmanship article or the tea-etiquette article because those articles have different editorial jobs.

The Jiuqu Stream and Riverside Tea Path records remain place pages. They are not converted into overview articles.

## Publication boundary found during audit

The current Natural Heritage, Global Tea History, and Zhu Xi bodies contain internal phrases such as “Draft boundary,” “the finished article should,” “the planned article,” and “before publication.” They are useful research drafts but are not finished visitor-facing articles. Phase 1B therefore preserves their stable slugs while removing them from public routes, public relationship links, and the sitemap until complete reviewed bodies replace the draft text.
