# Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing new material. It helps keep the website useful to international readers without replacing source review, fact checking, permission, or Jeff's personal confirmation.

Phase 1C article-by-article readiness findings are recorded in `docs/PHASE-1C-EDITORIAL-AUDIT.md`. That audit is internal and does not change publication status.

## Knowledge articles

- Why does this subject matter?
- Why should an international visitor care?
- What evidence supports the main claims?
- What local perspective does Jeff add?
- Are historical and scientific claims verified?
- Are evidence, interpretation, and personal observation clearly separated?
- Does the article link to relevant places, topics, videos, or stories only when those relationships are real?

## Places

- Where is it?
- Who is it suitable for?
- What practical information matters?
- What experience does it provide?
- Which details came from Jeff's personal experience, official information, or another source?
- Are changing conditions and unconfirmed details clearly marked?
- Are the English and Chinese names verified?

## Stories

- Who is this person?
- What specific experience tells the story?
- Does the story connect with a larger Wuyishan theme without claiming to represent an entire community?
- Does the writing avoid abstract praise?
- Does it use real, confirmed details?
- Has the person approved the interview, quotations, photographs, and video used on the page?

## Final review for every content type

- Does the English sound natural when read aloud?
- Does the page avoid literal translation and tourism marketing language?
- Are sources, dates, rights, and verification status complete?
- Are all relationship IDs valid?
- Is the record still a draft until the full review is complete?
- Does `npm run content:check` pass?
