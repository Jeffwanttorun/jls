# Phase 1C Editorial Readiness Audit

This is an internal editorial audit. It does not create, rewrite, approve, or publish article bodies. Public status, navigation, URLs, and sitemap behavior remain unchanged.

## Status definitions

- **Ready for review:** A complete body is present and can enter final editorial and source review.
- **Needs source verification:** A body exists, but important claims or terminology still need authoritative support.
- **Needs rewritten body:** The current body is incomplete or no publication-ready reviewed body has been supplied.
- **Needs additional research:** The article cannot be completed responsibly with the evidence currently recorded in the repository.

`Needs rewritten body` does not authorize generated copy. It means Jeff or the editorial workflow must supply a complete reviewed body before publication can be considered.
