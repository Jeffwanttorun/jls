Hi, I’m Jeff.

I live in Wuyishan and I share this place through videos, stories, and my own experiences.

I’m not here to show only the famous places.

I want to help visitors understand the landscape, culture, and people behind Wuyishan.

## Why I Share Wuyishan

I live in Wuyishan, and I want to introduce this place through research, stories, and personal exploration.

Most international visitors I meet know about the mountains or Da Hong Pao tea, but they still have basic questions: Which station should I use? Is that trail too steep? Where is the actual town? Clear answers in English can be hard to find. I started this site to explain those everyday details in plain English.

I’m a licensed tour guide in China. This website helps me share what I know about Wuyishan in clear, practical English.

If Wuyishan is completely new to you, begin with my [first-time Wuyishan travel guide](/wuyishan-guide). It explains where the city is, how people get here, and how the Wuyi Mountain scenic area fits into a trip.

## My English Journey

I’m learning English so I can help connect Wuyishan with people from different countries.

Travel information is one part of this project. Research, local stories, and what I notice in everyday life also help me find clear ways to explain why Wuyishan matters.

Building this website gives me a way to practice English while finding clearer ways to share Wuyishan with people around the world.

## What I care about

- Clear, practical advice grounded in firsthand experience
- Travel that respects local life
- Practical details for parents and first-time visitors
- Tea without pressure or complicated language
- Friendly conversations across cultures

## At the coffee cart

If you visit Wuyishan, you may find me at the coffee cart—probably making a drink and practicing my English.

A public contact method is being prepared and will be added to the website when it is ready.
