This website is a personal guide to Wuyishan. I want it to be useful without pretending that one person can keep every detail complete or current.

## What the labels mean

**Based on Jeff’s personal experience** means I’m writing from my own experience of living in Wuyishan.

**Checked in person by Jeff** means I checked the information during a visit to the place on the page. When a check date is available, the page shows it.

**Based on official information** means the detail comes from a clearly named official source, such as a park operator, transport provider, or government agency.

**Recheck before you go** means the information may change or still needs a current check. This is especially important for access, schedules, tickets, transportation, weather, and safety rules.

## How pages are updated

I use editorial dates and source labels when that information is available. If I haven’t confirmed a detail, I leave it blank instead of guessing.

Some older pages still need a detailed local review. The trust label on each page is there to make that limitation visible.

A reliable correction channel is being prepared. This section will be updated when it is available.
